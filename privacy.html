Privacy Policy – Life_app

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. YouTube API Services

Life_app uses YouTube API Services. By using Life_app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

2. Who uses this app

Life_app is a personal desktop tool used only by its developer to manage the developer's own YouTube channels. It is not distributed to or used by the public.

3. Information accessed, collected and used

  • YouTube Data API: uploading the developer's own videos, setting titles, descriptions, tags, captions, thumbnails, the altered/synthetic content disclosure and scheduled publish times; reading basic information of the developer's own channel (channel title, subscriber count).
  • YouTube Analytics API: reading performance data of the developer's own channel (views, watch time, average view duration, audience retention, audience age/gender, traffic sources) to create private reports.
  • Life_app does not access any other user's data, does not use cookies and does not collect device identifiers.

4. Storage, sharing and retention

  • OAuth tokens and reports are stored only on the developer's own computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Summaries of the developer's own channel statistics may be processed by an AI assistant chosen by the developer to write reports.
  • Statistics (views, likes, watch time and similar metrics) are kept for at most 36 months. Other data received from the API (for example video titles) is deleted or refreshed within 30 days.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Life_app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Using "Disconnect" in the app or deleting the app's data folder on the computer removes all stored tokens and reports. Deletion requests can also be sent to the contact below and are completed within 7 days.
