Life_app

Life_app is a private desktop tool used by a single creator to manage their own YouTube channels. It uploads videos that the creator made, schedules their publication time, and reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics data to prepare weekly and monthly performance reports.

The tool runs only on the creator's own computer. It is not offered to the public and does not access any other person's account or data.

Developed with YouTube – this app uses YouTube API Services.

By using Life_app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Features

  • Upload the creator's own videos and Shorts, with title, description, tags, captions, thumbnail and the altered/synthetic content disclosure.
  • Schedule the public release time of those videos.
  • Read the creator's own channel analytics (views, watch time, retention, audience) and create private weekly/monthly reports.
